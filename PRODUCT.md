# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS, no build, published with GitHub Pages from `docs/` in the public repo Laurent-stp95/steady (confirmed 2026-11-07). The app itself lives in Laurent-stp95/muscu-auto.

## Users

People who strength-train at home with their own equipment (rack or Smith machine, plates, dumbbells, kettlebell, bands, bench) and want a program they can trust without a coach. They train alone, phone in hand, between sets.

## Product Purpose

Steady builds the strength program by itself from the equipment you own, then makes it progress carefully: technique before load. Success is training regularly for months, getting stronger, without getting hurt.

## Positioning

Slogan: « Progresse sans te blesser ». The program only prescribes loads you can actually mount with your plates, and every progression rule favours clean technique over weight (learning sessions, reps before kilos, deload weeks, pain → exercise swapped).

## Operating Context

Installed on the iPhone home screen from Safari (web app, not the App Store), works offline, no account. Used in a home gym: series by series on the phone, optionally projected on a TV, voice and camera help.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Program from equipment: weekly split by days available, goal, level, duration; 52 curated exercises with French cues, muscle diagrams, images (wger, CC-BY-SA).
- Loads: only mountable loads, plates per side shown; progression: reps before a jump > 10 %, fixed weights → reps → tempo → pause → variant.
- Safety: 2 light learning sessions per new exercise, RIR ≥ 2 on compound lifts, "clean technique?" check, 5-week cycles + 1 deload, readiness check before each session (sleep, soreness, energy), pain → exercise replaced, comeback after a break (−10 % / −20 %).
- Session: one set at a time, auto rest timer, notifications screen locked, guided warm-up, express session, undo last set.
- Voice coach and voice commands; camera that counts reps (MediaPipe, on device); TV screen projection with phone camera mirror.
- Progress: charts, records, session history, weekly review, recovery map, push/pull balance, pain map. Backup and export.
- The landing page is a showcase: no link to the app itself and no install guide (decision 2026-11-07). Exception asked by the user the same day: the TV screen address (laurent-stp95.github.io/muscu-auto/tv/) is shown as a link with its 3 pairing steps, to show what the app can do.
- Licence: screenshots showing wger images or muscle diagrams carry a CC BY-SA credit in their caption (user decision 2026-11-07). The "test data" mention was removed with the footer at the user's request.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Steady. Slogan: « Progresse sans te blesser ».
- Icon: horizontal bar with a spirit level bubble, orange #c8441d on #1c1c1e (`muscu-auto/icon.svg`).
- French, informal "tu", plain language, no em dash, no emoji.

## Evidence on Hand

- The running app (screens can be captured), its icon, exercise images and muscle diagrams (wger CC-BY-SA, attribution required).
- No users, testimonials, ratings, download numbers or App Store presence: never invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Technique before load.
2. The program fits the equipment, never the reverse.
3. Nothing to configure during the set: the phone does the counting and timing.
4. Your data stays on your phone.
